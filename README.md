# Wonderrot

**A dark, bloody, psychological LitRPG choose-your-own-adventure through Wonderland, played inside a looking glass and narrated by a fourth-wall-breaking Cheshire Cat.**

> **Content warning:** violence, gore, psychological horror, death. Mature readers only.

## Episodes

| # | Title | Scenes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Long Fall | 284 |
| 2 | The Caterpillar's Smoke | 218 |
| 3 | The Duchess's Kitchen | 153 |
| 4 | The Mad Tea-Party | 135 |
| 5 | The Queen's Croquet | 110 |
| 6 | The Trial | 118 (five endings, one secret) |

`index.html` is a library that launches all six. Finish an episode and your **Glass Code** (a portable save string) is remembered and pre-filled in the next one. You can also paste a code by hand, or begin any later episode as a stranger.

## Features

- Branching, replayable story; deaths leave Reflections that unlock new options
- LitRPG progression: Nerve, Wit and Guile, Blood and Mind, levels, XP, gear, achievements
- A Codex of lore, a Satchel of mushrooms and teas, few but important weapons
- Choices carry across episodes: your minutes, favours, mercies and the names you gave
- Per-episode systems: Time's Minutes (Ep4), the Queen's Favour and the seven letters (Ep5), a twelve-juror trial with evidence and Rule 42 (Ep6)
- Gothic looking-glass UI, procedural WebAudio sound, mobile friendly
